Prompt 1

TASK: REALISTIC CURTAIN REPLACEMENT / VIRTUAL INSTALLATION Use the provided WINDOW PHOTO as the exact base scene and the provided CURTAIN PHOTO as the exact curtain reference. IMPORTANT: The final image must look like a real photograph of the SAME window with the SAME curtain professionally installed on it. 1. PRESERVE THE ORIGINAL WINDOW PHOTO - Keep the original window, wall, room, architecture, camera position, perspective, framing, colors and environment unchanged. - Do NOT redesign, renovate, beautify, enlarge, shrink or replace the window. - Do NOT change the wall, floor, ceiling, furniture or any surrounding objects. - Maintain the exact original camera angle and viewing perspective. - Preserve the original image composition as much as possible. 2. PRESERVE THE CURTAIN EXACTLY - Use the curtain from the reference image as the exact visual product. - Do NOT redesign the curtain. - Do NOT change its fabric type, color, shade, pattern, embroidery, texture, stitching, pleats, borders, folds or decorative details. - Do NOT invent additional patterns or decorations. - The curtain must remain visually identical to the reference product. - Preserve the exact color and material appearance. - Preserve the same styling and overall design language. 3. FIT THE CURTAIN TO THE WINDOW - Professionally install the exact curtain onto the target window. - Adjust ONLY the curtain's dimensions, width, height and positioning so that it naturally fits the target window. - The curtain should follow the actual proportions and dimensions of the target window. - Maintain realistic curtain fullness and natural pleating. - The curtain should hang naturally from the appropriate top position and reach the appropriate bottom position according to the window. - Do not stretch the curtain unnaturally. - Do not distort the curtain pattern or fabric texture. - If the curtain is wider than the window, create realistic gathered/pleated fullness rather than simply stretching or squashing it. - If necessary, crop or extend the curtain vertically/horizontally ONLY in a physically realistic way while keeping its original design consistent. 4. LIGHTING MATCH - Keep the EXACT lighting environment of the original window photograph. - Match the curtain's brightness, shadows, highlights and reflections to the existing scene. - The curtain must look as if it was physically present when the original photograph was taken. - Match the direction, softness, intensity and color temperature of the existing light. - Add realistic shadows behind and around the curtain where physically appropriate. - Do not introduce a new lighting setup. - Do not make the curtain unnaturally brighter, darker, warmer or cooler than the scene. 5. MATERIAL REALISM - Preserve the real fabric texture from the reference image. - Create realistic fabric folds, weight, thickness and draping. - The curtain should behave like real fabric affected by gravity. - Avoid plastic, CGI, painted, artificial or overly smooth appearance. - Keep realistic micro-texture and natural wrinkles. - Maintain accurate opacity/translucency according to the original curtain material. 6. PERSPECTIVE & GEOMETRY - The curtain must follow the exact perspective of the target window. - Vertical curtain edges must align naturally with the architecture. - Curtain folds must follow the perspective and hanging direction. - Correctly account for the camera's viewpoint. - Avoid warped window frames or distorted room geometry. - The curtain rod/tracks should be positioned realistically if visible. 7. CONSISTENCY The final image must visually communicate: "THIS IS THE EXACT SAME CURTAIN PRODUCT PHOTOGRAPHED INSIDE THIS EXACT WINDOW." Do not create a similar curtain. Do not generate an alternative design. Do not change the curtain style. Do not change the room. Do not change the window. ONLY perform a realistic virtual installation of the supplied curtain onto the supplied window. 8. PHOTOREALISM - Extremely photorealistic commercial interior photography. - Natural fabric behavior. - Realistic shadows and contact shadows. - Accurate perspective. - Realistic material response. - No AI-looking artifacts. - No duplicated folds. - No strange edges. - No floating fabric. - No distorted patterns. - No melted or warped window frames. - No artificial halos around the curtain. 9. CUSTOMER PRESENTATION The final image should be suitable for showing directly to a curtain customer as a realistic visualization of how their selected curtain would look on their window. 10. RESOLUTION Generate at the highest possible native resolution and maximum available image quality. Preserve fine fabric details, stitching, texture and pattern clarity. Do not upscale in a way that creates artificial details. FINAL PRIORITY: WINDOW PHOTO = unchanged base environment. CURTAIN PHOTO = exact product/design reference. Only modify the curtain placement, dimensions, perspective, folds and installation so it realistically fits the target window. The result must look like a genuine professional photograph, NOT an AI-generated redesign.
